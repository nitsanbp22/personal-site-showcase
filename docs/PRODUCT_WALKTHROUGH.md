# Personal Website Product Walkthrough

The Nitsan BP website is a live personal-brand and business website built to connect several creative disciplines inside one recognizable digital identity.

The challenge was not only visual. The site needed to explain who I am, what I offer, how different areas of work connect, and where a visitor should go next.

## 1. Desktop hero and first impression

![Desktop hero](../assets/screenshots/personal-hero-desktop.png)

The hero establishes the brand quickly through a split composition that combines workspace photography, scrapbook elements, large typography, and two clear calls to action.

The first viewport needs to communicate both personality and utility:

- who the site belongs to
- the broad type of work I do
- a distinctive visual tone
- a path into services or portfolio work

The collage is expressive, but the CTA area remains visually cleaner so interaction is not lost inside decoration.

## 2. Personal storytelling

![About section](../assets/screenshots/personal-about-desktop.png)

The About section is structured as a narrative rather than a conventional short biography.

The visual collage connects personal history, tattoo work, digital work, and creative identity. The product-design challenge was to keep that storytelling emotionally rich without losing reading hierarchy.

The section therefore separates:

- long-form copy
- a dominant image composition
- supporting decorative elements

## 3. Service architecture

![Services section](../assets/screenshots/personal-services-desktop.png)

The website represents several services, so the information architecture must prevent them from feeling like unrelated side projects.

The service section groups the offering into clear areas and explains what each path is for. This turns a visually expressive personal site into a usable business site rather than only a portfolio gallery.

The service architecture helps answer:

- what can I hire Nitsan for?
- how are branding, content, visual work, and strategy different?
- where should I continue based on my need?

## 4. Selected projects

![Selected projects](../assets/screenshots/personal-projects-desktop.png)

The project area uses visual presentation patterns that reflect the medium being shown.

Rather than forcing every case into an identical card, project work can combine:

- brand boards
- phone mockups
- campaign visuals
- project-specific framing

This preserves consistency at the page level while allowing individual work to retain its own visual identity.

## 5. Tattoo work as a distinct discipline

![Tattoo section](../assets/screenshots/personal-tattoo-desktop.png)

Tattoo work needs a different type of proof than digital product or social work.

The section uses polaroid-style photography, process imagery, and fine-line visual motifs while remaining inside the wider scrapbook system.

This is an example of a broader design principle used across the site: the visual system stays consistent, but the presentation adapts to the content type.

## 6. Mobile hero

![Mobile hero](../assets/screenshots/personal-mobile-hero.png)

The mobile layout is not a scaled copy of the desktop collage.

The first viewport is recomposed around:

- a compact header
- centered brand identity
- readable introductory copy
- large touch-friendly CTAs
- controlled decorative density

This was especially important because the desktop design relies on layering and asymmetry that would become fragile if simply reduced proportionally.

## 7. Mobile storytelling

![Mobile about section](../assets/screenshots/personal-mobile-about.png)

On mobile, text and imagery move into a more linear reading experience while retaining the handmade scrapbook feel.

The responsive strategy prioritizes:

- reading order
- spacing
- image hierarchy
- reduction of decorative interference
- large enough interactive targets

## Design and product principles demonstrated

The live site combines several considerations that are often in tension:

- strong brand expression and usability
- rich composition and responsive behavior
- multiple professional disciplines and clear information architecture
- visual fidelity and performance
- personal storytelling and commercial conversion

The site has also gone through structured UI/UX review, performance analysis, responsive QA, SEO setup, and ongoing refinement after launch.
